About the Farmhouse

Ringelův statek is a historical farmhouse from 1820 with typical features of a Broumov classicist estate. Don't expect a shiny modern guesthouse – our house has its years and minor flaws, but it breathes a genuine, authentic countryside atmosphere.

You will find us in a beautiful and quiet place in the village of Vernéřovice in the Broumovsko protected area. Right from the garden, there is a wonderful view of the surrounding meadows and forest. The house is an ideal place for a holiday for 2 to 3 families or a group of friends. Inside, a large vaulted hall, a spacious living room with a fireplace, and four cozy bedrooms await you.

Well-deserved relax under the open sky

Imagine an evening after a full-day trip in the rocks. The water is a pleasant 38°C, the air smells of burning wood, and you look at the peaceful nature with a glass of wine. Our high-quality outdoor hot tub is the ideal place to wash away the tiredness of the whole day.

  • Built-in massage jets (whirlpool)
  • Year-round operation even in winter
  • Authentic and fragrant wood heating
  • Peace in our fenced garden

Tips for trips

Adršpach Rocks

Famous rock town with fascinating formations and boat rides. 25 min drive.

Teplice Rocks

Wilder neighbor of Adršpach. Deep gorges, steep walls and fewer tourists.

Broumov Walls

Beautiful Hvězda viewpoint and Kovářova gorge. A quieter alternative.

Broumov Churches

A unique group of baroque churches by the Dientzenhofer builders in the area.

Table Mountain Ostaš

Great for families. Upper and Lower Labyrinth full of rock mazes for kids.

Wenet Farm

Animal paradise in Broumov. Llamas, camels, ostriches and more animals.

Broumov Monastery

Historical pearl of the region with a library, garden and a great cafe.

Bischofstein Castle

Romantic castle with a restaurant, castle ruins and a mysterious lake.

Walzel Center

Ideal for rainy days (6 km). Švejk restaurant, bowling and a climbing wall.
