About the Farmhouse

Ringelův statek is a historical farmhouse from 1820 with typical features of a Broumov classicist estate. Don't expect a shiny modern guesthouse after a complete reconstruction – our house has its years and minor flaws, but it breathes a genuine, authentic countryside atmosphere.

You will find us in a beautiful and quiet place almost at the end of the village of Vernéřovice. Right from the garden, there is a wonderful view of the surrounding meadows and the nearby forest. The house is an ideal place for a holiday for 2 to 3 families or a group of friends. Inside, a large vaulted hall with a dining table, a spacious living room with a cozy fireplace, and four bedrooms await you.

Well-deserved relax under the open sky

Imagine an evening after a full-day trip in the rocks. The water is a pleasant 38°C, the air smells of burning wood, and you look at the peaceful nature with a glass of wine. Our high-quality outdoor hot tub is the ideal place to wash away the tiredness of the whole day.

Tips for trips

Adršpach Rocks

Famous rock town full of fascinating formations, waterfalls, and boat rides. Located just 25 minutes by car from us.

Teplice Rocks

Larger and wilder neighbor of Adršpach. Deep gorges, steep walls, and far fewer tourists await you here.

Broumov Walls

A quieter but equally beautiful alternative. Don't forget to visit the Hvězda viewpoint and the picturesque Kovářova gorge.

Broumov Churches

A unique architectural ensemble of baroque churches by the famous Dientzenhofer builders scattered across the villages.

Table Mountain Ostaš

A great choice for families with children. Kids will love the Upper and Lower Labyrinth full of rock mazes.

Wenet Farm (Broumov)

An animal paradise that will thrill especially the youngest visitors. You will meet llamas, camels, ostriches, kangaroos, and lemurs.

Broumov Monastery

The historical pearl of the region. The monastery offers a breathtaking library, family tours, and a great cafe in the garden.

Bischofstein Castle

A romantic little castle with an excellent restaurant, the ruins of Skály castle, and the mysterious Black Lake.

Walzel Center

An ideal "wet option" when it rains (only 6 km). Here you will find the excellent Švejk restaurant, bowling, and a salt cave.
